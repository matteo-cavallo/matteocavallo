For years, I've worked as a frontend engineer, building interfaces for large-scale projects. I've learned precision, structure, and how to make complex systems feel simple.

But somewhere along the way, I started craving something more personal — something that could reflect not just what I do, but why I do it.

This site is where that idea starts to take form.

It's not a portfolio in the traditional sense. It's a living space — a place to experiment, to connect dots between design and engineering, to explore the balance between logic and emotion.

I'm learning to move slower. To build with intention. To make things that last.

This space will grow with me — changing shape as I do.

## Vision

The compass for how I build, think, and live.

1. **Good work can change things.** For people, for communities, for the world around us.
2. **Clarity matters.** I believe in quiet design, in beauty as a form of truth.
3. **Purpose over performance.** What we make should leave the world — even slightly — better.
4. **People first.** The quiet power of collaboration, empathy, and care.
